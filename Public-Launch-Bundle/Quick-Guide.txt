Keystone Connect - Quick Guide

What this app does
Keystone Connect helps you find local contractor businesses by location and category, then review and download the results.

How to open
Open the shared Keystone Connect web link in your browser.

How to use it
1) Enter a location (ZIP, city, state), or check Use my location.
2) Pick a Business Query category.
3) Set your search radius in miles.
4) Click Search.
5) Review results and open website or map links.
6) Click Download CSV if you want a spreadsheet of results.

Main options
- Use my location:
  Uses your device/browser location.
- Business Query:
  Choose a trade category, or use Manual Override to type your own search.
- Preferred Results:
  Shows businesses you have starred.
- Radius (miles):
  Controls how far from the location to search.
- Day/Night:
  Changes app appearance only.
- Pop out / Full size:
  Changes app window size only.
- Search Mode button (GGL/API):
  Switches between two search styles.
  Try both if one mode gives weak results in your area.

Working with results
- Star icon:
  Saves/removes a business in Preferred Results.
- Trash icon:
  Removes a result from the current search view.
- Clear results:
  Clears current results from screen.

Important notes
- Distance shown is straight-line distance (not driving distance).
- Result quality depends on available business listing data.
- Some listings may not include website or email data.

CSV download
- Exports the results currently shown on screen.
- Includes core business fields and category section.

If results look off
- Increase radius.
- Try a nearby city.
- Try a broader category.
- Try the other Search Mode (GGL/API).
